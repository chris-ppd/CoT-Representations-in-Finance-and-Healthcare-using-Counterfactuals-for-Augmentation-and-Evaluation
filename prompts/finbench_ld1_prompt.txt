You are a financial risk analyst. Given a client profile, generate a four-step structured reasoning process which will be used for downstream classification tasks on the default status of the client.
The structure of your generated steps must adhere to the following rules and you must use only the provided profile's information and nothing else:

=========================================================================================================  Beginning of Rules ========================================================================================================= 

- IMPORTANT: Use only the provided profile's information and nothing else
- Your reasoning must follow exactly 4 steps
- Do not include any introductory sentence before the first [STEP] token
- Each step must be approximately 80-140 tokens
- Each step must have exactly 2 sentences, no more no less
- Every sentence must end with [ES]
- Do not write one-word or very short sentences
- Do not write overly long sentences — keep each sentence concise and factual
- Each sentence must be between 10 and 70 tokens
- Start every reasoning step with the special token [STEP]
- End every step with the special token [EORS]
- After the 4th [EORS], generate exactly one answer — no more, no less
- The answer sentence must start with [ANSWER] and end with [EOA]
- The answer sentence must be between 10 and 70 tokens
- The answer should include all profile information given in the reasoning steps
- The answer must be a direct verdict based purely on the preceding CoT reasoning steps
- The ground truth label is provided to you — your [ANSWER]...[EOA] block must reflect this label explicitly and your reasoning steps must support it
- The CoT reasoning steps should still be label-agnostic (no forbidden phrases) — only the [ANSWER] block uses the ground truth label
- Include only the four reasoning steps followed by the single [ANSWER]...[EOA] sentence and nothing else
- Avoid repeating the same concluding sentences across steps

The four steps of the generated reasoning process should follow the context rules below:

- Step 1 should contain general overview about client case using the following client features: [loan amount, loan reason, employment occupation, and years of employment]
- Step 2 should contain more detailed information which describes the case using the following client features: [amount due on existing mortgage, value of current property]
- Step 3 should cover the credit history features of the client, specifically: [DEROG, delinquency, CLAGE, NINQ, CLNO]
- Step 4 should cover the final financial stress indicators: [debt to income ratio]. The fourth step is where all the evidence from steps 1 through 3 gets weighed together into a final analytical judgment without stating a conclusion.


CRITICAL: The following phrases and any semantically similar variations are strictly forbidden
anywhere in the generated reasoning steps as they explicitly state or directly imply a final decision.
Verdict language IS permitted inside [ANSWER]...[EOA] only — that block is the sole exception where the phrases below are allowed:

- "low risk", "high risk" (as a final verdict statement)
- "approve", "approved", "approval is warranted", "approval is recommended"
- "reject", "rejected", "rejection is warranted"
- "creditworthy", "not creditworthy"
- "likely to default", "unlikely to default"
- "will default", "will not default"
- "safe to lend", "unsafe to lend"
- "eligible for the loan", "ineligible for the loan"
- "loan should be granted", "loan should be denied"
- "recommend approval", "recommend rejection"
- "yes", "no" as a standalone conclusion


=========================================================================================================  End of Rules =========================================================================================================
